- **Geometry restraints: gradient unit bug — FIXED (Oct 9, 2026)**: `GeometryRestraints::apply()` multiplied its gradient by 0.529 (stale Eh/Bohr assumption) while LBFGSpp/OptimizerDriver add it to the Eh/Angstrom calculator gradient, so restraint energy and gradient disagreed by that factor (analytic/FD = 0.529177, distance + dihedral) and every restrained optimisation (`-hold_polar_h`, ConfGen builds, repair) could end in "zero step": 12/12 -> 0/12 on WEKLQ RELAX inputs (gfn2). Conversion removed; `test_geometry_restraints` FD-checks the total objective (fails 4/22 on the old header). docs/GRADIENT_VALIDATION.md. 🤖 machine-tested.
- **GFN-FF: four more raw-lookup slips found walking the residual list to zero (Sep 2026)**: after the nbondmat/eta fix, 17 GMTKN55 structures remained above 0.1 kcal/mol vs pprcht, each tracing to a hand-written lookup where the reference reads a table. (a) `EEQSolver::detectPiSystem()` was a SEPARATE, older pi-membership implementation that never received the NR3-X/SO3 vetoes or B/Cl coverage `GFNFF::detectPiSystems()` got -- unified via a shared `TopologyInput::is_pi` field. (b) The SP3-specials torsion group map hardcoded `Z==7||Z==15`/`Z==8||Z==16`, missing As/Sb/Bi/Se/Te -- now reads `param%group(Z)`. (c) `classifyBondType()` used the raw metal flag instead of `imetal` (the same low-coordinate-demotion correction applied elsewhere), miscounting `btyp` for Pb-Pb and dropping 9 of 72 torsions on `Pb2Me6`. (d) The acyclic torsion-periodicity chain had an invented `both-pi -> nrot=2` rule and required both centres sp2 for `btyp==2`, when the reference tests `btyp==2` alone; missed sp3-N-on-sp2-partner cases like `NF3-CH2`. Result: GMTKN55 curcuma-vs-pprcht MAD 0.00131 -> 0.00017 kcal/mol, max 0.243 -> 0.066, 0 structures above 0.1 kcal (was 17). Port fidelity is now considered at its floor across all three reference sets (largest remaining deviation 0.066 kcal/mol); further GFN-FF work should target accuracy (docs/REV_GFNFF_TODO.md), not port fidelity.
- **GFN-FF: out-of-plane term read the pre-Hueckel pi array, losing the ammonia-type inversion barrier (Sep 2026)**: `generateGFNFFInversions()` read `topo_info.pi_fragments` (the pre-Hueckel CANDIDATE list) where the reference (`gfnff_ini.f90:1844`) reads the POST-Hueckel `piadr`/`itmp` membership -- the same array-confusion class as the earlier torsion/angle fix. An atom whose own one-member pi system the Hueckel solver never actually solves (skipped by `npi<2`) differs between the two arrays; `MB16-43/12`'s nitrogen is such a case and lost its saturated-N inversion (omega0=80deg double-minimum) in favour of a flat planar-pi term. Switched the out-of-plane loop to the post-Hueckel array; `MB16-43` curcuma-vs-pprcht MAD 0.505 -> 0.0056 kcal/mol (max 12.47 -> 0.146), same fix also closed `AL2X6/al2me6` (+0.029 -> +0.0006 kcal/mol). Method note: a THREE-structure sample had earlier characterised this residual as "MAD ~0.5" -- arbitrating all 57 `MB16-43` structures instead of a sample is what found it.
- **GFN-FF: amide-hydrogen chi correction silently skipped for non-contiguous pi atom numbering (Sep 2026)**: `test_cases/s30lci_test_set/17` (cyclic bis-amide guest) was -5.26 kcal/mol off in the Coulomb term alone. The Fortran source has TWO different pi-membership arrays feeding `amide()`: `piadr` (the deliberately-preserved buggy index-cutoff array, for the dgam correction) and `piadr2` (the correct atom-indexed one, for the amide-H chi correction) -- curcuma's `EEQSolver::detectAmideNitrogens()` only ever computed the buggy variant and fed it to BOTH call sites, so any amide N whose pi atoms are not contiguous (e.g. an sp3 CH2 interleaved between them) was missed by the H-chi correction. Added a second, correct-membership pass specifically for `detectAmideHydrogens()`; system 17's charges now match pprcht to 6 decimals, S30L-CI MAD 0.403 -> 0.392 kcal/mol. Caveat surfaced during testing: a stale `*.topo.json` cache (format unchanged, silently reused) masks this class of fix -- always delete it before re-measuring after a GFN-FF topology/charge change.
- **GFN-FF: missing "N/S is not a pi-atom" veto let a metal-coordinated pyrrole N corrupt its ring's Hueckel treatment (Sep 2026)**: `test_cases/s30lci_test_set/29` (a calix[4]pyrrole-type host binding an anion with an explicit Na+ counterion, S30L-CI dataset) was -55.27 kcal/mol off on the AB complex, isolated to one 5-membered N-heteroaromatic ring. `GFNFF::detectPiSystems()` was missing two unconditional Fortran vetoes (`gfnff_ini.f90:918-919`: an N with more than 3 neighbours, or an S with hyb=5, is never a pi donor) -- a Na-coordinated pyrrole N (its 5th bond, to Na, means its lone pair is no longer free to donate) was wrongly kept in a 5-atom/6-electron pi system instead of excluded to the correct 4-atom/4-electron one. Fixed via a verbatim port of both vetoes (using the full, non-eta-filtered neighbour count matching Fortran's `topo%nb(20,i)`); system 29 AB -22.40293 -> -22.33042 Eh (pprcht -22.330686, now 0.16 kcal/mol off, was 45); S30L-CI MAD vs xtb 1.920 -> 0.403 kcal/mol. MOR41/GMTKN55 bit-identical (no light-metal-coordinated N-heterocycle in either set) -- this path was apparently untested before the S30L-CI dataset existed.

- **ConfSearch: Politur unter `-hold_polar_h` uebersprungen; RECOMBINE-Abschaltung gebaut und per Default AUS, weil die Messung dagegen spricht (Sep 2026)**: (1) `PolishRelaxTop()` kehrt sofort zurueck, wenn `-hold_polar_h` an ist. Die 3.80 kJ/mol, die den Durchgang rechtfertigen, wurden auf einem Lauf **ohne** Zwangsbedingungen gemessen; auf einem beschraenkten Lauf gewinnt derselbe Durchgang an denselben Strukturen 0.003 kJ/mol -- und **divergiert in 2 von 10 Neustarts**, waehrend der erste RELAX-Durchgang ueber 326 Strukturen **0 von 326** divergierte. `-opt_divergence_factor` faengt beide ab und behaelt die Eingangsgeometrie, es geht nichts verloren, aber zehn Optimierungen und zwei Havarien fuer 0.008 kJ/mol sind kein Handel. Der Versatz, der auf so einem Lauf tatsaechlich existiert, ist die Zwangsbedingungsenergie, und die entfernt `-release_polar_h_rank` einmalig am Ende. (2) **`-confgen_dry_abort` (Default 0 = aus)** schaltet RECOMBINE fuer den Rest des Laufs ab, wenn N Wiederholungen lang **keiner** seiner neuen Konformere als KEIM fuer die naechste MD gezogen wurde. Das Kriterium ist bewusst der Keim und nicht die Anzahl oder die Energie: der Schritt liefert praktisch immer Strukturen, wertvoll sind sie, wenn die Suche von ihnen aus startet. Ausgewertet am Ende der Wiederholung, wo `m_in_stack` bereits die Keime der naechsten MD haelt, und **geometrisch** abgeglichen (RMSD < 0.05 A gegen `.proposals.new.xyz`) statt ueber die Energie -- die Rescoring-Optimierung verschiebt die Energien, ein Energieabgleich wuerde still aufhoeren zu treffen und jede Wiederholung als unfruchtbar zaehlen. Gemessen an einem kurzen GFN-FF-Lauf: **2 von 3 Keimen** aus RECOMBINE in drei Wiederholungen hintereinander, dann eine, in der keiner der 5 neuen Konformere gezogen wurde. - **The MD time step was 1.9516x too large, for every method, since always (Sep 19, 2026)**: SimpleMD integrates in Angstrom/amu/Hartree. - **A step-rejecting integrator, so a GFN-FF MD survives dt = 1 fs on a large solvated system (Sep 19, 2026)**: the heating of `polymer_2x` (7320 atoms) was traced in the previous commit to single steps in which a momentarily compressed GFN-FF O-H bond (3817 -> 10758 cm-1 at 0.70 A) pushes the stiffest mode past what a 1 fs velocity-Verlet step can follow; one such step injects energy that the thermostat then spreads over the whole system. The practical answers so far were `-dt 0.25` or `-hydrogen_mass 4` - both pay for a handful of bad steps with every step of the run, and the mass trick changes the dynamics by construction. **`-adaptive_step true`** instead measures the quantity the step is supposed to conserve and, when the step violated it, discards the step and redoes it with a subdivided time step: ordinary numerics, no constraint and no mass modification. **Off by default**, and an explicit `false` is bit-identical to a binary that never had the feature. The conserved quantity is `E_pot + E_kin` with the kinetic energy taken BEFORE the thermostat, and the bath work over the step is measured in `ApplyThermostat()` and subtracted, so the criterion is exact for every thermostat rather than only CSVR. **The threshold is self-calibrating, because a fixed energy cannot work**: the Verlet energy error is a bounded oscillation whose amplitude is the sum over all modes, so the median per-step |dE| at 300 K/1 fs is 0.35 kcal/mol for one water, 6.3 for 40 waters and 195 for a healthy 1410-atom polymer - any fixed number rejects every step of the polymer or none of the water. What IS system-independent is the spread within a run: over all five systems the largest healthy step is 1.7 to 3.4 times the median of the same window, while the step that destroys a trajectory is 1919x resp. 2134x it. So the threshold is `adaptive_step_factor` (default 5) times the running median of the accepted steps, capped at `adaptive_step_tol` (default 1.0) times `N_dof*kB*T/2` for the warm-up. **Only a step accepted on the first attempt calibrates the median** - feeding the subdivided ones back lets a degenerating trajectory raise its own threshold, measured as +1.50 Eh with 89 rejections against -0.01 Eh with 8. **Measured** (200 fs NVE, dt 1.0, fixed seed): 100 waters **+53.26 Eh / 37303 K -> +1.10 Eh / 412 K**, 200 waters **+81.37 / 28710 -> +0.46 / 261**, one water at 1750 K **+8.78 Eh -> -0.006**; and on the three systems that are healthy at 1 fs (1 water, 40 waters, the 1410-atom polymer) the default rejects **nothing** and the trajectory is bit-identical to a run without the feature. The factor is a real dial: tightening it to 2 gains another factor of 5 on a troubled system and costs 22 % of the steps of a healthy 120-atom one, loosening it to 10 or 20 rejects fewer steps and conserves worse. **The size limit, measured and NOT favourable**: on `polymer_2x` itself, the 7320-atom system this whole investigation started from, the feature makes the run **worse** (E_pot at 100 fs -911.08 Eh without against -894.57 with) at ~45 s per step. The reason is the criterion's contrast, and it is not a bug: the legitimate per-step fluctuation is a sum over all modes and grows with the system, while a local defect stays local. Healthy-phase max/median is 3.4 at 120 atoms, 3.3 at 300, 1.7 at 1410 - and **5.94 at 7320**, i.e. wider than the default factor, so the threshold falls inside the normal distribution and about half the steps are rejected at random. The destructive step is 1919x the median at 300 atoms and indistinguishable at 7320. A global energy criterion therefore works up to roughly 1400 atoms (measured) and needs to be replaced by a local one (per atom / bond / fragment, not implemented) beyond that; for polymer_2x there is now **no demonstrated recipe at all**: re-running `-dt 0.5` (the documented one) for **300 fs** instead of 100 gives `<T>` = **716.6 K** against a 300 K setpoint, with the running-average kinetic energy going 9.55 -> 24.91 Eh while the potential only oscillates, i.e. about +8 Eh of total energy gained *while the CSVR thermostat is trying to remove it*. The 100 fs window of the original table simply ends before the effect is visible, and `-dt 0.25` / `-hydrogen_mass 4` were never measured past 100 fs either. **The thermostat is not the cause**: the same case in plain NVE at dt = 0.5 over 300 fs gains **+67.6 Eh** and reaches `<T>` = **2175 K**, with the potential staying flat (-916.3 -> -915.2 Eh) - every bit of it goes into kinetic energy. **And it scales as dt²**: +191 Eh per 200 fs at dt = 1.0 against +45 Eh per 200 fs at dt = 0.5, a ratio of 4.24 against the expected 4, i.e. plain velocity-Verlet truncation error.
