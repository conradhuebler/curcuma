# Wall-time comparison: curcuma native QM vs ORCA 6.1 and PySCF 2.14 (Oct 2026)

> 🤖 AI-generated, ⚙️ machine-tested. One machine, one run per number (n = 1), no repetitions, not reviewed by the
> operator. The numbers hold for this CPU (hybrid P/E cores), this GPU and the settings below; they are not a general
> ranking of the programs.

Scripts: `scripts/qm_timing/` (`geometries.py` structures, `qm_timing.py` runner and parser, `pyscf_job.py` one PySCF
job, `report.py` tables, case lists `cases_serial.txt`, `cases_parallel.txt`, `cases_extra.txt`, driver `run_all.sh`).
Raw results stay local: `~/.cache/qmtime/results.jsonl`, logs in `~/.cache/qmtime/logs/`.

## Short answer

- **HF on the GPU** (`-gpu cuda -qm.gpu_direct on -qm.gpu_scf on`, exact integrals): 8-14x faster than ORCA with 24
  MPI processes and 7-50x faster than PySCF with 24 threads from anthracene to C80H162 (1930 basis functions: 38 s vs
  471 s ORCA, 268 s PySCF, 712 s curcuma CPU).
- **HF on the CPU, exact integrals**: with 24 threads 1.4-1.5x slower than ORCA from C20H42 on (per SCF iteration about
  equal, curcuma needs 16 instead of 11 iterations), 1.6-2.7x slower than PySCF from C40H82 on. With 1 thread 4-5x
  slower than ORCA (anthracene, C20H42): the CPU integral code is the weak point.
- **RI-JK HF**: curcuma is the fastest of the three up to C20H42 (CPU 1T and 24T), but above 16 GB of 3-index tensor
  the default switches to `batched` recomputation and becomes slower than ORCA and PySCF (C40H82 238 s vs ORCA 337 s,
  PySCF 135 s; C80H162 over 30 min). With `-qm.ri_max_memory_mb 200000` C40H82 takes 96 s (CPU) / 53 s (GPU).
- **RI-MP2**: total time is mostly the SCF time. 24 threads: 0.5-1.4x the ORCA time (benzene 0.08x, ORCA start-up),
  GPU variant 6-17x faster than ORCA. 1 thread: 3.8-5.4x slower than ORCA from anthracene on, the exact SCF accounts for ~80 %. RI-MP2 stops with an error from C40H82 on
  (tensor above the default 16 GB `ri_max_memory_mb`).
- **Local MP2** (curcuma `lmp2` vs ORCA DLPNO-MP2): equal wall time on the CPU with 24 threads (C20-C80), GPU variant
  4-6x faster; the correlation step itself is 10x faster in curcuma (C80H162 31 s vs ~357 s), the run is dominated by
  the SCF. Error vs canonical RI-MP2: curcuma 21-24 µEh/atom, ORCA NormalPNO 34-41, TightPNO 17-21.
- **CCSD**: with 1 thread all three programs are within 0.86-1.25x of each other; with 24 threads curcuma is the
  fastest ((H2O)12: 1088 s vs ORCA 1560 s, PySCF 1288 s), although its BLAS is the sequential MKL.
- **Energies** agree within the SCF/CC convergence chosen: HF within 5e-9 Eh of PySCF (curcuma) and 1e-7 (ORCA, energy
  criterion only), RI-MP2 E_corr within 2.3e-8 (curcuma) and 5.6e-8 (ORCA), CCSD within 2.8e-8 (curcuma) and 5.2e-8 (ORCA).

- **PySCF 2.14.0**, numpy 2.5.4, scipy-openblas64 0.3.34 (OpenMP via `OMP_NUM_THREADS`, BLAS via

  anthracene (D2h, r(CC) 1.40 Å), all-trans n-alkanes CnH2n+2 (r(CC) 1.53, r(CH) 1.09 Å, tetrahedral), (H2O)n on a
  cubic 2.9 Å grid.
- **Load**: other jobs were not running (checked with `top` and `nvidia-smi`; an unrelated C120 curcuma job of another
  session ran during the first three serial jobs only (curcuma HF 1T benzene, anthracene, start of C20H42; 1 core). The 1-min load average
  before the 1-thread jobs was 0.94-1.79 (median 1.03, the idle baseline of this desktop is about 1). Before the
  24-thread jobs it is 6-44, which is the tail of the previous 24-thread job of the queue (jobs run back to back), not
  a foreign load.
- **Thread setting**: 24 threads = `-threads 24` (curcuma; sets the QM OpenMP threads), `%pal nprocs 24` +
  `%maxcore 4000` (ORCA, MPI), `OMP_NUM_THREADS=24` (PySCF). 1 thread = the same with 1, plus `taskset -c 2` (one
  P-core) for all three programs, ORCA `%maxcore 64000` without `%pal`.
- **Wall time** = the whole program run measured by the runner (start-up, integrals, SCF, correlation, output), not the
  programs' internal timers. ORCA with `%pal` has ~7-9 s fixed start-up cost (MPI start of every module); that is part
  of its wall time and dominates its small cases.
- **Cap**: 1800 s per job; a size whose predicted time (previous size times (n_bf ratio)^p, p = 2.5 HF, 4 RI-MP2,
  1.5 LMP2, 5.5 CCSD) exceeded 2700 s was skipped ("pred"); after a timeout ("cap") or failure the larger sizes of that
  series were skipped.

## Fairness rules and what differs anyway

- Same geometry (one file per structure for all programs), basis def2-SVP (spherical; curcuma's `def2-SVP.dat`, PySCF's
  library and ORCA's built-in set give the same HF energies to < 1e-8 Eh), closed-shell RHF.
- **def2-TZVP not run**: the native engine has no f functions (`createGTOFromBasis`: "f/g shells not supported").
- **Auxiliary basis**: def2-universal-JKFIT (= ORCA `def2/JK`) everywhere: RI-JK (`-qm.ri jk`, ORCA `RIJK def2/JK`,
  PySCF `density_fit(auxbasis="def2-universal-jkfit")`) and the correlation fit (curcuma default `-qm.ri_mp2_aux`,
  ORCA `%basis AuxC "def2/JK" end` (ORCA warns "Weird basis set assignment"), PySCF `DFMP2.with_df` with the same name).
  curcuma ships no def2-SVP/C, so this is a method variant, not standard RI-MP2/def2-SVP/C.
- **Frozen core** on in all correlated runs (1s of C and O): curcuma `-qm.frozen_core true`, ORCA default, PySCF `frozen=n_heavy`.
- **SCF convergence** (not identical by construction): curcuma `-qm.scf_threshold 1e-7` (Frobenius norm of the density
  change), ORCA `TightSCF` (TolE 1e-8; ORCA stopped on the energy criterion with RMS density above its 5e-9), PySCF
  `conv_tol 1e-9`, `conv_tol_grad 1e-5`. Iterations for the alkanes: curcuma 16, ORCA 11, PySCF 9 (different guesses
  and DIIS settings); this is part of the measured difference.
- **CCSD convergence**: curcuma `-qm.cc_threshold 1e-8 -qm.cc_residual_threshold 1e-7`, ORCA `%mdci STol 1e-7`,
  PySCF `conv_tol 1e-8`, `conv_tol_normt 1e-6`.
- **Exact HF path** is each program's default: curcuma stores the ERI tensor below 4000 MB (`eri_max_memory_mb`; only
  benzene and the small water clusters) and runs integral-direct above; ORCA is integral-direct (`NoRI`); PySCF keeps
  the ERIs in memory if they fit `max_memory` 64000 MB (benzene to C20H42) and runs direct above (C40H82, C80H162).
- **LMP2 vs DLPNO-MP2 differ by design** (domains, PNO thresholds, pair screening); only the error against canonical
  RI-MP2 (curcuma RI-MP2 with the same aux basis) is comparable, plus the time. ORCA DLPNO-CCSD is listed for context only.
- curcuma's `total HF energy` line has 8 decimals; its HF deviations of a few 1e-9 are partly that rounding.
- curcuma GPU variants: `gpu_direct` = `-gpu cuda -qm.ri off -qm.gpu_direct on -qm.gpu_scf on`; `gpu_rijk` =
  `-gpu cuda -qm.ri jk`; for RI-MP2/LMP2 `gpu` = `-gpu cuda` only (defaults, see the trap below). The GPU FP32 phase
  (`-qm.gpu_eri_precision auto`, Q-G5) was on in all GPU runs; final energies are FP64.

## Assessment

**Where curcuma is competitive or ahead**

- Exact HF on the RTX 5090 (integral-direct, MD kernels, FP32 early phase): 8-14x ORCA/24 processes at every size from
  anthracene to C80H162. This is the only path where curcuma is clearly ahead for large systems.
- RI-JK HF while the 3-index tensor fits the default 16 GB (up to C20H42): faster than ORCA and PySCF on 1 and 24 threads.
- Canonical CCSD: equal with 1 thread, ahead with 24 threads (0.22-0.76x ORCA, 0.47-1.04x PySCF). ORCA's MPI
  parallel canonical CCSD scales poorly here (water10: 1800+ s 1T vs 571 s 24 processes); curcuma's OpenMP over
  sequential-MKL GEMMs scales 4-6x on 24 threads (P+E cores).
- LMP2 correlation step (local fit, integral-direct 3-index): ~10x faster than ORCA's DLPNO-MP2 step at C80H162, with
  an error between NormalPNO and TightPNO.

**Where curcuma is slower, by what factor, and likely reasons**

- **Exact CPU integrals**: 1 thread 4-5x slower than ORCA and 2-11x slower than PySCF (anthracene 219 s vs 43 s vs 20 s);
  per SCF iteration ~3x ORCA (C20H42: 31 s vs 10 s). The two-electron integral and J/K digestion code of the CPU path
  is the bottleneck (it is the reference implementation the GPU kernels are checked against, not tuned). With 24
  threads the per-iteration time equals ORCA's, so the gap there is the iteration count (16 vs 11; dP criterion
  1e-7 vs ORCA's energy criterion, guess, DIIS start) and parallel scaling hides the kernel cost.
- **SCF iterations**: 16-19 for alkanes/anthracene vs 9-11 in ORCA and PySCF; a cheaper convergence test (energy
  plus a looser density criterion) or a better guess would save ~30 % everywhere, including the GPU path.
- **RI-JK above 16 GB tensor**: `ri_storage auto` switches to batched recomputation of the 3-index integrals in every
  J/K build (C40H82: 88 s of 228 s J/K is integral recomputation); 2.5x slower than the stored tensor. A default that
  takes the machine's free memory into account would avoid it; at 1 thread both curcuma and ORCA RI-JK hit the cap at C40H82.
- **RI-MP2 at C40H82 and above**: stops with an error at the default `ri_max_memory_mb` (needs the stored tensor, no
  batched/integral-direct RI-MP2). The MP2 energy step itself scales only 3.4x from 1 to 24 threads (C20H42 44 s ->
  13 s).
- **GPU auto path trap**: with `-gpu cuda` and the defaults (`gpu_direct auto`, `gpu_eri true`) `QMEngine::ensureGpuJK`
  first builds the full Q-G2 ERI host tables and only then finds that the n^4 tensor cannot fit the card: +8 s at
  C40H82 (HF wall 15.2 s vs 7.5 s with `-qm.gpu_direct on`), ~+160 s at C80H162 (LMP2 GPU run: "SCF" 182 s, SCF loop
  20 s). The RI-MP2/LMP2 `gpu` columns include this; `-qm.gpu_direct on` avoids it (TODO.md, section 2).
- **Single-thread BLAS**: the release binary links sequential MKL; curcuma parallelises with its own OpenMP over
  sequential BLAS calls. For CCSD that works (scaling 4-6x), for the RI-MP2 energy step and the RI-JK batched path less so.

**Caveats**

- One machine, n = 1, hybrid CPU: thread placement over P and E cores is left to the OS for 24-thread runs.
- PySCF with 24 threads is slower than with 1 thread for benzene (4.5 vs 1.3 s) and scales only 2x for C20H42 in core
  (118 vs 237 s), while its integral-direct path at C40H82 is fast (96 s). OpenMP and OpenBLAS threads were both set to
  24; nested oversubscription (load up to 44) is likely and was not tuned. The PySCF 24-thread numbers may be pessimistic.
- ORCA was used out of the box (TightSCF, default integral thresholds, no `!Conv`, no RIJCOSX); ORCA's HF energies
  drift to +1e-7 Eh at C80H162 because it stops on the energy criterion. The ~8 s MPI start-up is included.
- The skip-by-prediction rule is approximate (e.g. ORCA 1T C80H162 HF predicted 2723 s and was skipped, curcuma 1T
  C40H82 HF predicted 2704 s); those cells are estimates of "over the cap", not measurements.
- RI-JK and RI-MP2 use def2/JK as correlation aux basis in all programs; standard def2-SVP/C would give other timings.

### GPU default path fixed (Oct 25, 2026)

tensor (n^4 * 8 B plus the plugin's margin of 3 n^2 matrices and 256 MB) with the card's total memory before
it builds any host table; only tensors below that size still build the tables, and the plugin then checks
the free memory as before. Measured with `-gpu cuda` and otherwise the HF flags of this study (24 threads,
